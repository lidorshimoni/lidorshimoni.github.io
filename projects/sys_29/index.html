// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered an artificial intelligence agent to master platform navigation without human hand-crafting (`Icy-Tower_NEAT_AI`). Rebuilt the mechanics of Icy Tower from scratch in Python using PyGame, including player momentum, gravity, dynamic camera scrolling, and procedural platform placement. Integrated the NEAT (Neuroevolution of Augmenting Topologies) genetic algorithm, exposing sensory inputs (relative platform distances, player velocities, screen bounds). Evaluated populations across hundreds of generations, allowing complex network topologies (nodes and recurrent connections) to evolve naturally while visualizing speciation and fitness progress via Graphviz.
