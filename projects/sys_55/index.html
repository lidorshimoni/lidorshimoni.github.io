// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Implementation of a Variational Autoencoder (VAE) in PyTorch. Trained on the CelebA celebrity-faces dataset and on an anime-faces dataset, generating 64x64 samples. The repository includes the model definition (VAe.py), a dataset loader (dataset.py), and example outputs after 100 epochs on CelebA and 60 epochs on the anime dataset.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Generated CelebA faces after 100 epochs of training.
  • ›Generated anime faces after 60 epochs of training.
  • ›Separate dataset loader module (dataset.py).
