// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered an autonomous aerial robotics platform for waypoint navigation and aerial surveying. Centered around a Pixhawk 32-bit autopilot running ArduCopter firmware. Employs a U-blox M8N GPS receiver with an integrated digital compass elevated on a carbon fiber mast to isolate it from motor electromagnetic interference. Configured a 433MHz 500mW MAVLink telemetry radio transceiver linking the drone to a ground control station running QGroundControl and Mission Planner, executing fully automated takeoff, multi-point coordinate waypoint routes, geofenced boundaries, and failsafe Return-To-Launch (RTL).
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Configured EKF3 sensor fusion integrating accelerometer, gyro, barometer, and GPS data.
  • ›Executed autonomous GPS waypoint survey grids with sub-meter position hold accuracy.
  • ›Programmed automated battery voltage and RSSI fail-safes with return-to-launch procedures.
  • ›Live ground station HUD displaying airspeed, heading, altitude, and artificial horizon.
