// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered and piloted high-agility RC aircraft utilizing paper-faced foam board and the score-and-fold technique (popularized by FliteTest). Utilized precision 50-percent score cuts through the outer paper and foam to fold monocoque fuselage boxes and curved high-camber leading edges. Beveled 45-degree hinge cuts with hot-melt glue and fiber packing tape form durable, flutter-free control surface hinges for elevons. Equipped with an 1806 2300KV motor in a protected pusher configuration, enabling rapid combat flight, tight loops, and high survivability.

  • ›Cost-effective platform for aggressive low-altitude maneuvers and pilot training.
