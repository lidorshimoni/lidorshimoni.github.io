// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered and flight-tested a high-performance fixed-wing aircraft constructed entirely from 3D-printed lightweight PLA (LW-PLA). Sliced with single-perimeter 0.4mm continuous extrusion with foaming temperature tuned to 235°C, reducing material density by 45% compared to standard PLA. Designed internal gyroid and geodesic structural spars directly into CAD to resist high-G aerodynamic bending and torsional flutter. Features interlocking fuselage bayonets reinforced with carbon fiber pultruded rods, integrated micro-servo bays for elevons, and an aerodynamic pusher motor nacelle.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Optimized thin-wall single-perimeter slicing for exceptional rigidity-to-weight ratio.
  • ›Carbon fiber spar integration prevents wing fold during high-speed pull-outs.
  • ›Modular interlocking sections allow rapid field replacement of damaged nose or wing tips.
  • ›Extensive flight testing verified stall speed and roll rate stability.
