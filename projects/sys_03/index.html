// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Built a continuous-operation diffusion cloud chamber to detect subatomic particles at atmospheric pressure without liquid nitrogen or dry ice. Employs a multi-stage stack of TEC1-12706 thermoelectric Peltier modules coupled to a heavy aluminum CPU liquid-cooling block, driving the black anodized baseplate below -30°C. 99.9% isopropyl alcohol evaporates from a heated top felt reservoir and descends through a steep thermal gradient, forming a supersaturated vapor zone. A ~1.5kV electrostatic clearing grid (salvaged from a fly zapper) sweeps background ions from the active volume. Introducing radioactive isotopes (Americium-241 from smoke detectors, Thorium-232 from lantern mantles, and Uranium-238 autunite ore from eBay) produces vivid ionisation condensation tracks showing distinct thick alpha lines and tortuous beta curls.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS

  • ›Documented distinctive trajectory characteristics of heavy alpha vs. light beta particles.
  • ›Optimized thermal isolation with dual-pane acrylic chamber walls.
