// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
MicroArm-35 is an active Guinness World Record attempt for the smallest robot arm. The parametric CAD design targets a 35mm maximum dimension - measured on the arm itself, independently of its controller and base - using 2g sub-micro servos carried over from the Tiny Titan build and resin 3D printing for sub-millimeter assembly tolerances. The Guinness application was filed in October 2026. This is an attempt in progress: no record is held or claimed.
