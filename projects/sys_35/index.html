// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
A series of lithophane prints: photographs converted into variable-thickness relief surfaces that reveal the image only when backlit. Includes a spherical moon lamp combining a cratered lunar shell with an embedded photo lithophane, and flat portrait panels tuned for layer height, wall thickness and light diffusion on an FDM printer.
