// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Fabricated an efficient, high-aspect-ratio RC soaring glider utilizing custom thermal cutting tools. Constructed an adjustable tension wooden bow strung with 0.5mm nichrome resistance wire, powered by a variable DC bench power supply calibrated to the precise sublimation temperature of extruded polystyrene (XPS). Guided the wire along laser-cut aluminum airfoil templates (Clark-Y and Selig profiles) with tapered root-to-tip chord progression. Reinforced the foam wings with embedded carbon fiber strip spars and bi-directional fiberglass tape, producing an ultra-light, smooth wing with exceptional lift-to-drag ratio.
