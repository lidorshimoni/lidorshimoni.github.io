// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
A WD 2TB portable drive with a soldered USB interface (no native SATA) stopped enumerating. Diagnosed a failed controller PCB, sourced a matching donor board, and transferred the original flash ROM chip - which stores drive-unique adaptive data - onto the donor PCB before imaging the volume. The teardown covered head-stack and platter inspection, corrosion found under the EMI shield, and the ROM transplant itself.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Flash ROM transfer preserved drive-unique adaptive data across the board swap.
