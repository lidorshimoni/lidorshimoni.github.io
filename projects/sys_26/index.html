// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Designed and fabricated a permanent laboratory bench power supply tailored for electronics prototyping. Integrated a heavy toroidal step-down transformer followed by low-noise linear regulators providing fixed reference rails (+3.3V, +5V, ±12V) with ultra-low ripple for sensitive analog and RF circuits. Added dual high-efficiency DC-DC buck-boost converters with ten-turn precision wirewound potentiometers delivering 0-30V continuously variable outputs with adjustable current limits (0-10A). Equipped with bright 4-digit LED volt/amp displays, short-circuit crowbar protection, and thermal fan speed controllers in a ventilated grounded steel chassis.
