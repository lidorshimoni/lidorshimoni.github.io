// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered a classic Spark Gap Tesla Coil (SGTC) to study high-frequency resonant transformers. Powered by a high-voltage neon sign transformer (NST) charging a custom polypropylene plate pulse capacitor bank. The primary circuit features a flat pancake spiral wound from heavy 6mm copper tubing and an adjustable multi-segment quenched spark gap with forced fan cooling. The secondary resonant coil consists of over 1,000 turns of 32 AWG enameled copper wire wound around a 110mm PVC cylinder, terminated with a spun aluminum toroid capacitor. At resonance (~250kHz), the system generates brilliant 30cm+ electrical corona breakout arcs and wirelessly lights gas-discharge tubes.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Precisely tuned primary and secondary LC tanks to exact quarter-wave resonance.
  • ›Engineered quenched multi-gap spark array with forced air cooling to prevent arc latching.
  • ›Demonstrated wireless electromagnetic energy transmission lighting fluorescent tubes 2m away.
  • ›Constructed RF strike rail ground to protect primary components from flashovers.
