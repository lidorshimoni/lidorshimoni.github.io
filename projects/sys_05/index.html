// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered a high-current battery tab spot welder for assembling 18650 and 21700 lithium-ion battery packs. Salvaged two identical microwave oven transformers (MOTs), removed the high-voltage secondary coils using a reciprocating saw and punch, and rewound them with 3 turns of heavy 35mm² (2 AWG) ultra-flexible copper welding cable. Connected the secondary windings in parallel to sum current while maintaining ~3.8V open-circuit voltage. Integrated a microcontroller-based dual-pulse timing circuit controlling a 100A SCR (silicon-controlled rectifier) firing a 15ms surface pre-heat pulse followed by a 45ms forge pulse, delivering flawless welds on 0.15mm-0.2mm pure nickel tabs without heating the battery cell.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Machined transformer laminations and rewound heavy gauge multi-strand copper cable.
  • ›Dual-pulse timing eliminates electrode sparks and prevents battery terminal burn-through.
  • ›Foot-switch actuated pneumatically damped handheld welding pen.
  • ›Fabricated dozens of custom high-capacity Li-ion power packs for drones and electric bikes.
