// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Constructed an electrochemical plating setup for surface functionalization of metals and 3D prints. Formulated copper sulfate acid baths, Watts nickel electrolyte baths, and zinc solutions with organic brightening additives. To plate non-conductive plastic 3D prints (PLA/resin), developed a surface preparation technique using ultra-fine conductive colloidal graphite spray to establish electrical continuity. Implemented a temperature-controlled, magnetically stirred plating tank powered by a precision constant-current DC power supply to ensure uniform ion migration and dense, mirror-bright metallic coatings.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Engineered reliable electroplating on 3D printed parts for metallic appearance and EMI shielding.
  • ›Calibrated current density (mA/cm²) to eliminate dendritic nodules and burning.
  • ›Achieved high-adhesion mirror-finish chrome-like nickel coatings on functional prototypes.
  • ›Safely managed chemical neutralisation, wastewater filtering, and ventilation.
