// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Designed and fabricated a high-aperture Newtonian reflector telescope utilizing generative design and FEA structural optimization in SolidWorks. Evaluated deflection gradients along the optical tube assembly (OTA) under varying elevation angles to minimize primary-to-secondary collimation drift. Features a 3D-printed 9-point flotation primary mirror cell, a 3-vane curved spider mount to eliminate classical 4-point diffraction spikes on stars, carbon fiber truss rods, and a zero-backlash printed helical focuser with standard M42 threads for DSLR astrophotography.
