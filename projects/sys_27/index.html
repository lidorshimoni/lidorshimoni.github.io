// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Converted a discarded cathode ray tube (CRT) color television into an analog X-Y vector oscilloscope. Safely discharged the 25kV anode button, disconnected the stock horizontal line-sweep flyback circuit, and separated the vertical and horizontal magnetic deflection yoke coils. Designed an impedance-matching interface feeding both yoke coils through dual Class-AB high-current audio power amplifiers. Feeding stereo audio signals or function generator outputs into the channels directly deflects the electron beam in two axes, painting brilliant green/white Lissajous figures, vector music synthesizers, and real-time audio waveforms directly onto the phosphor faceplate.

  • ›Installed safety bleeder circuits and isolated input transformer stages.
