// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Designed and built custom CoreXY 3D printers from raw materials. Built on 2020/2040 V-slot aluminum extrusions with precision CNC-machined corner plates, utilizing high-precision MGN12H linear guide rails on all axes. CoreXY kinematics keep both NEMA 17 stepper motors stationary on the frame, drastically reducing moving carriage mass. Equipped with a custom direct-drive BMG dual-gear extruder, an all-metal bi-metallic heatbreak capable of continuous 300°C+ extrusion for engineering polymers (ABS, ASA, PC, Nylon), a 230V AC silicone heated bed on cast tooling plate, and a Raspberry Pi running Klipper firmware with accelerometer-tuned input shaping and pressure advance.
