// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Designed and built to challenge the Guinness World Record for the smallest humanoid robot (then 141mm; at 95mm it was 32% smaller, but a 57.676mm robot claimed the record before verification concluded - attempt concluded, no record held). Powered by 6 ultra-miniature 2g sub-micro servos and an all-in-one WitMotion 16-channel Bluetooth PWM driver board stepped up from dual 200mAh 1S LiPo cells. When vendor software proved inadequate and restricted, reverse-engineered the serial protocol by packet sniffing, identified the 5-byte motor command packet, and bypassed Android restrictions by reverse-engineering Nordic UART Service (NUS) over BLE on iOS. Authored and published two open-source Python drivers on PyPI enabling full wireless teleoperation.
