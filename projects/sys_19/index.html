// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Upgraded the capabilities of the budget FlySky FS-i6 2.4GHz AFHDS 2A transmitter through embedded firmware modding. Connected an ST-Link V2 programmer directly to the transmitter mainboard SWD (Serial Wire Debug) header to flash an open-source patched firmware. Expanded the stock 6-channel limitation to 10 fully proportional channels mapped to auxiliary switches and potentiometers. Enabled digital RSSI error rate reporting on the primary LCD, multi-stage flight timer alarms, and configured FS-iA6B/iA10B receivers to stream single-wire digital iBus telemetry directly into flight controller UART ports.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Overrode factory manufacturer channel restrictions without buying expensive transmitters.
  • ›Enabled single-wire serial iBus protocol to eliminate cable clutter to drone flight controllers.
  • ›Configured audio alarms for real-time radio link quality degradation.
  • ›Fully documented wiring pinouts and flashing steps for the maker community.
