// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Engineered an experimental high-voltage discharge apparatus to explore electrical breakdown mechanics and Lichtenberg figure formation in solid dielectrics. Driven by a 2.2kV / 500mA microwave oven transformer (MOT) feeding stainless steel probe electrodes. Wood specimens are prepared with a saturated sodium bicarbonate (baking soda) electrolytic solution. When voltage is applied, current follows paths of least electrical resistance, boiling the moisture, pyrolyzing the cellulose into conductive carbon channels, and propagating branching fractal patterns that mimic lightning strikes and river deltas.
