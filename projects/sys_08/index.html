// ENGINEERING_NOTES & IMPLEMENTATION
Constructed an electric foundry capable of reaching 1150°C for casting non-ferrous metals. Built inside a reinforced steel drum lined with 50mm high-density 1400°C ceramic fiber blanket, rigidized with colloidal silica and coated with high-alumina refractory mortar. The heating element consists of hand-wound Kanthal A1 resistance wire embedded in internal ceramic grooved muffle walls, drawing 2.5kW at 230V AC. Temperature regulation is governed by an industrial REX-C100 PID controller with a high-temperature Type-K stainless thermocouple driving a 40A solid-state relay (SSR) with zero-cross switching.
// KEY_INNOVATIONS & BENCHMARKS
  • ›Achieved stable 1100°C melts without gas cylinders, burner tuning, or noise.
  • ›Custom coiled Kanthal element with calculated surface watt density for long coil life.
  • ›Cast precision aluminum machine brackets, lathe blanks, and custom artistic bronzes.
  • ›Implemented secondary thermal cutoff fuse and ground fault protection.
